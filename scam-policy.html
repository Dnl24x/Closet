SAFETY

Scam & Safety Policy.

A doua șansă is designed to keep marketplace conversations together so buyers and sellers can make safer decisions.

Effective October 5, 2026

1. Keep communication on A doua șansă

Use the in-site messaging system for purchase discussions. Be cautious when someone asks you to move a conversation to another service, follow an outside payment link, or continue a deal away from the marketplace.

2. Never share security information

Never send another person your password, one-time verification code, recovery code, session token, or other account security information. A legitimate marketplace conversation should never require another member to receive your login credentials.

3. Be careful with payment requests

Do not trust urgent requests, fake support messages, suspicious payment instructions, or screenshots presented as proof that a payment was made. Confirm the transaction inside the A doua șansă flow and keep records of the conversation.

4. Check the listing and the seller

Review the listing details, price, photos, seller profile, location and conversation before agreeing to a transaction. Be especially careful with unusually low prices, pressure to act immediately, requests for unusual payment methods, or stories designed to make you ignore warning signs.

5. Offers stay inside the marketplace

Use the Give Offer feature rather than sending an offer through an external link. An offer is a negotiation request, not proof that a purchase or payment has happened.

6. Report suspicious messages

Save the relevant conversation details and contact A doua șansă through the Contact page when you see suspected fraud, impersonation, threats, or suspicious payment requests.

7. What A doua șansă will never ask you to do

We will not ask you to reveal your password or one-time verification code. We will not ask you to bypass marketplace safety controls. Be suspicious of anyone claiming to be staff while asking for secrets, money, or a move to an external service.
